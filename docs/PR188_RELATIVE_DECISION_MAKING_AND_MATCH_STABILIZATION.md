# PR188 — Relative Decision-Making & Match Behaviour Stabilization

## 1. Findings and readiness

PR188 is an incremental experimental stabilization. It removes absolute risk admission
as a substitute for choosing between difficult football actions, prices a short horizon
of inaction, and separates a preparatory intention from actual release. Normal matches
now establish a lawful kick-off. Human free kicks have current-position quick options
and a separate physically travelled preparation path.

**B remains the default. No policy is promoted. Canonical remains NOT READY for
summary calibration, and the independent confirmation cohort remains unopened.**
Short controlled episodes are useful falsification attempts; they do not certify an
entire match, all defensive controllers, or empirical psychological realism.

**Full-match falsification:** all three revised experimental policies still exhibit
severe stationary episodes in the frozen WW design. Opportunity-Driven reaches
1,984.7 seconds (about 33 minutes); Hierarchical Intent reaches 436.75 seconds and
Contextual Judgment about 476 seconds. Same-source B stays below five seconds in
that design. The local fix therefore does **not** satisfy full-match liveness or
attacking-creation acceptance. This is a draft with negative development evidence,
not a candidate for promotion or a claim that weak-player inactivity is solved.

## 3. Stationary possession: causes and limits

The old candidate values combined absolute skill error, lane geometry and lost value,
then three separate policies applied hard risk/confidence envelopes. Ordinary weak
passes could be rejected before comparison with waiting. Holding stayed available,
with a cost based mostly on current nearest-player pressure. A second approach and
ball-side access were poorly represented. Several locally feasible options therefore
lost to repeated protection even when the physical situation was deteriorating.

The missing-preparation fallback also used the current observation time. Adding an
action's preparation demand to a fresh timestamp could keep postponing that action.
The fallback now uses the carrier's actual ownership start. A valuable future option
can produce a **preparation intention**, which turns the body through existing
movement and leaves actual readiness/contact authoritative.

These are verified local mechanisms. The unavailable human files prevent assigning
every twenty-minute observation to one cause. Stable moving protection, tactical
delay, a physically occluded ball, and an expired receiving lane remain alternatives.
The revised full WW traces also record thousands of preparation intentions and
poor pass completion. That points to a remaining interaction between preparation,
contact, moving opportunities and defending; aggregate reason counts alone cannot
establish which dependency causes each long episode. A same-input diagnostic repeat
records actual scene, preparation, pressing plan and challenge eligibility at fixed
stationary-duration thresholds, with the final physical/runtime hash compared.
The repeat confirms that the longest 1,984.7-second episode is **open play**, with
no active restart. At 20, 60 and 1,200 seconds of stationary possession, the copied
scene has no current contact although its preparation timestamp is already past;
at the 300-second sample contact is available. The nearest defender remains about
2.27 m from the carrier / 2.79 m from the ball, in containment with no legal contact
candidate. The subjective waiting risk stays 0.26. These snapshots localize an
unresolved preparation/contact/engagement interaction; they do not justify expanding
physical reach, forcing a pass or treating a prediction as actual pressure.

## 4. Relative opportunity representation

The typed copied scene contains own execution attributes, observed geometry/velocity,
uncertain individual opponent memory, and separate tactical/psychological context.
It contains no opponent technical ratings or authoritative future outcomes.

Passing keeps an absolute technical-error estimate. Anticipated opposition uses the
local lane, travel horizon, observed movement and uncertain mobility. Carrying uses
the local interception corridor. Waiting projects 1.2 seconds of approach, body-side
protection, and a second angle of intervention. Uncertainty is recorded explicitly.
These are cheap **subjective heuristics**, not calibrated physical probabilities.
Repeated canonical trials are objective experiment outcomes, not training labels
generated from the decision evaluator itself.

Execution still belongs to movement, feet, preparation, ball flight, contact and rules.
No rating-difference formula awards success, changes reach, transfers possession, or
alters technical attributes. There are no quotas, forced turnovers or mandatory passes.

## 5. Four laboratory protocols and results

`benchmarkPr188.mjs` records paired mirrored 40:40, 60:60, 80:80, 40:80 and 80:40
conditions across eight situations and three experimental policies: 240 variants.
Active variants run 12 seconds at 0.025 s; passive variants stop at the original
possession's physical end. Seeds, complete inputs, candidate assessments, decisions,
attempts, actual pass/challenge diagnostics, body separations, facing errors,
pressing intentions and physical result hashes are archived.

Laboratory A additionally runs canonical prescribed pass, reception, turn, dribble
and timed-header exercises at 40 and 80. B includes unequal physical standing-contact
trials, labelled as prescribed contacts rather than autonomous tackle choices.
These complement, rather than replace, the autonomous C episodes. Physical abilities
can produce different execution results even at equal relative tiers.

Laboratory D holds physical teams/geometry fixed and varies eight score/time/briefing/
recent-evidence contexts, two mental profiles and psychology on/off. Its 32 initial
choices and two-second physical continuations are separate measurements. The research
archive also includes 62 physical trials, two learning sequences and nine wall trials.

In the three paired prescribed passing trials, mean initial error is 0.448 m at 40
and 0.322 m at 80. Both tiers execute all six timed headers; reactive-feint escapes
are 2/9 and 3/9 respectively. Those small prescribed samples demonstrate retained
execution differences; they do not estimate match success probabilities.

## 6. Decision liveness and policy distinctions

Opportunity-Driven still prioritizes useful expiring windows. Hierarchical Intent
still maintains/cancels a supported objective and compares bounded local alternatives.
Contextual Judgment still uses subjective near-tie discretion and individual preferences.
They now share comparative consequences rather than an absolute risk veto.
Existing controlled contrasts still produce different explainable selections.

Hierarchical Intent had another local failure: continuing supported progression could
hide a better visible finishing window. It now inspects a bounded set of finishing
options, cancels that continuation when the comparison warrants it, and can select
the existing finish objective. There is no shot bonus or shooting quota. Regression
tests check both the objective revision and a physically released shot.

Physical impossibility and offside remain gates. Low confidence is not impossibility.
An unready release may select preparation, never a fabricated kick. The observer
records that reason. Hold/carry remain legitimate when preferable or when contact is
not currently available. A preparation horizon is not a timeout forcing release.

## 7. Defensive engagement and pressing

The audit found a comfort/reach mismatch: a weak defender's comfortable contact
distance could be below the body separation maintained by movement, despite the
ball being exposed inside the resolver's existing 0.95 m standing reach. A covered,
aligned, exposed ball can now justify an imperfect standing attempt. Bookings,
rear approaches, speed, cover and physical access still matter. A containment plan
no longer rejects a comfortable exposed contact merely because several players cover.

An exposed reachable ball can also override an obsolete screen assignment. Orbital
approach, duel locks and all physical technique radii remain unchanged. These new
**decision preferences are staged in the three bounded policies**. Legacy/B/R1/R2/R3
retain their historical defensive selection behavior, sharing the same resolver.
The historical controller still reads full state; it is not certified bounded perception.

The final 30 human-retain episodes contain a physical challenge in 30/30 PR187 cases
and 27/30 PR188 cases: recorded outcomes are respectively 21/15 misses, 18/12 fouls,
6/9 clean wins and 0/3 beaten defenders. Several attempts can occur in one episode.
This apparatus therefore does not reproduce a universal baseline non-engagement
defect; neither attempt counts nor the three cases without a challenge prove success
or failure without their movement and possession-ending context.

## 8. Attacking depth and forward positioning

Full-match observers sample striker depth relative to the opponent's second-last
player and own midfield, with team phase and identity. Absolute x alone cannot compare
mirrored teams. Formation, role, transition, screening and cover explain some depth
differences; a forward is not universally instructed onto the last line.

PR187's bounded pattern adapter can cancel on insufficient visible cover. Its view
may omit distant defenders, so missing _observed_ cover is not proof of absent real
cover. This remains a limitation. No unverified global striker-depth correction is
applied simply to increase shooting. Full-match depth measurements are included below.

## 9. Opponent knowledge and uncertainty

Temporary per-observer memory starts from a neutral uncertain prior, not hidden OVR.
Visible starts/turns and distinct visible ball contacts update bounded mobility/control
expectations slowly. Duplicate contacts and unchanged ticks are not independent evidence.
One misleading fast episode cannot produce immediate certainty. The current decision
resistance principally uses mobility; control/tackling inference is not claimed calibrated.

Knowledge is individual, serializable and separate from persistent scouting. There is
no global team knowledge update. Public/scouted pre-match individual priors, richer
reaction/keeper evidence and bounded communication of those estimates remain future work.

## 10. Psychological context

The module is optional and **off by default**. It separates tactical urgency from
individual composure/determination, approximate coach briefing and recent personally
observed evidence. Event keys are deduplicated in bounded memory. Score never edits
opponent technical estimates, own attributes, contact reach or physical execution.
Small bounded discretion changes can alter a close choice; a changed result is not
proof of psychological realism. Persistent career-level confidence is not added.
The D laboratory deliberately injects recent-evidence and briefing counterfactuals;
those values are experimental inputs, not reconstructed personal match histories.

## 11. Actual kick-off

`createTacticalMatch()` establishes a stationary unowned ball at the centre mark and
legal initial bodies, then enters the existing live restart lifecycle. Initial setup
is before physical history; later half-time/post-goal restarts retain real positions
and travel normally. Authorization, taker contact, flight and receiving ownership
remain separate. Home begins the first half; away begins the second, as before.

Historical open-play fixtures explicitly use `createOpenPlayMatchFixture()`. Tests
and three standalone apparatus adapters preserve their declared starting geometry;
they do not mask the new constructor. New regressions independently check time zero,
lawful positioning and the first actual contact without invented receiving ownership.

## 12. Human quick/prepared free kicks

When legally available, **“Możliwe szybkie wznowienie”** presents current teammate
positions, including lateral/backward passes. Potential wall geometry alone is not
a referee whistle instruction for the human taker. An explicit ceremonial instruction
and disciplinary restrictions remain authoritative. A numerical tolerance fixes the
1.4 m boundary without increasing a physical contact radius.

**“Poczekaj na ustawienie drużyny”** selects no kick: canonical time resumes, bodies
travel, readiness/progress is shown, and an updated menu returns to the human. Readiness
uses actual legalized movement targets, not obsolete static zones. The watchdog observes
slow preparation; **“Zagraj z obecnego ustawienia”** provides explicit recovery only
when legal restrictions are satisfied. There is no elapsed-time auto-kick.

Quick selection does not rewrite geometry to future ceremonial positions. Prepared
crosses require the intended player near the presented zone. Selection freezes the
existing human decision snapshot/clock; subsequent execution resumes ordinary physics.
Cancellation and save/reload remain available; no new shooting confirmation layer is added.

Rules checked against current [IFAB Law 13](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/free-kicks/)
and [Law 8](https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/the-start-and-restart-of-play/).
A legal quick-kick interception by an opponent still inside 9.15 m is allowed by Law 13.
The existing direct/indirect, second-touch, wall, location and penalty-area rules remain
in force. Tests exercise the legacy law suite and actual live quick-pass contact.

## 13. Wall geometry and physical interference

The engine separates body centres at 1.15 m; existing wall slots use 1.3 m. Reducing
that constant alone conflicts with separation and readiness. The wall remains a
physical collection of bodies with ordinary deflections/jump responses; there is no
guaranteed shooting corridor. Wording now says **“Spróbuj wykorzystać widoczną lukę”**.

The controlled 2/3/4-body wall matrix checks available low/curl/gap profiles and actual
flight, responses and shot results. Unavailable techniques and misses remain recorded.
This does **not** validate camera/perceived body width equivalence or justify a tighter
wall. Coordinated lateral movement and better visual/body-envelope consistency remain
open rather than being hidden by shrinking a spacing constant.
In this specific nine-case series three techniques are unavailable and all six
offered shots physically release but miss over the goal. There is no successful
gap/under-wall outcome to use as validation of the wall or forecast model.

## 15. Negative evidence and unresolved blockers

Missing human exports, reconstructed geometry and a small development cohort constrain
causal claims. Shot forecasts, keeper behavior, full bounded defensive/restart controllers,
team communication, tactical learning and coordinated attacking depth remain incomplete.
Wall width consistency and subjective forecast calibration are not solved by this PR.
Development results never authorize opening confirmation or summary calibration alone.

Earlier failed/superseded apparatus, verify attempts and source revisions are indexed.
A selected intention is not a release; a release is not a completed pass or a good attack.
Stationary duration and continuous ownership are reported separately, and a long moving
protection episode needs its own diagnosis rather than automatic classification as a bug.

## 16. Determinism, cost and compatibility

Decisions use deterministic state/seeded observation, never wall-clock randomness.
The observer receives detached data. Complete observer-off laboratory replay checks
input and final physical/runtime hashes. Memory is bounded by identities and small
event records; there are no per-candidate full-state rollouts. Validated runtime data
is not reparsed wholesale at every decision/scan. Old saves get schema defaults.

Historical policy IDs, default B, the 28-attribute schema, canonical movement/contact,
substitution, stamina, injury, statistics, discipline and human ownership are retained.
CPU/RSS/wall receipts name their source. Historical wall-time comparisons are descriptive,
not a controlled speedup claim, especially when other checks ran concurrently.

The final serial observer-disabled comparison uses the same runner, 30 mirrored
arrived configurations and identical physical inputs on frozen PR187 and PR188.
Measured process CPU is 1.492 versus 1.410 ms per canonical tick; final RSS is
329.7 versus 344.9 MB. Actual tick totals differ (14,004 versus 13,608) because the
episode outcomes/early endings differ. The normalized CPU ratio is 0.945, but one
ordered pair with different subsequent work cannot establish that prediction became
cheaper or that the engine gained a statistically reliable speedup. Raw receipts
and limitations are in `performance/PR188-performance.json`.

The full WW/A diagnostic repeat has identical complete final state **values**.
Its original raw checksum check fails because JSON rehydration changes key insertion
order only in `departedPlayers[8]`. That failed receipt/log remains intact. A separate
comparison recursively sorts object keys, preserves all array ordering/values and
confirms an equal canonical SHA-256. This is distinct from the 240 laboratory
observer-off repeats, which preserve their original raw final-state hashes exactly.
