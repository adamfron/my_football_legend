## Baselines and ablations

`canonical-pr199` disables experiments explicitly and runs the current shared baseline. `historical-pr198-s` retains the prior combined recovery/restart/transition/clearance/attention/receiving/tempo plus D/H/R/S/loft settings, with canonical arrival false. It is a negative comparison, not a promoted candidate.

`realism-candidate-no-belief-v0`, `-no-defence-v0`, `-no-height-v0` and `-no-loft-v0` remove one declared component. Policy-specific inactive settings remain visible; changing an inactive flag is not evidence that acting football changed. Local same-ball branches and complete-match no-belief/historical contrasts are retained separately.

Initial v1 measurements, transitional v2 and final v3 candidate manifests retain exact source archives. V3 freezes auxiliary switches, historical missing-policy behaviour and known movement commitments. A deferred schema initialization correction is guarded by current-source equivalence replays of the final physical hashes. Exact archived replays remain available with `--historical-source`; old results are not rewritten to fit final metadata. The final checksum is computed by the resolver, not maintained manually in Markdown.

## Promotion decision

The complete frozen receiver set reproduces R154/123/116 and S149/113/108. Incoming-facing yields155/117/112: better than S, worse than R in control/retention. Fresh90-ball results remain mixed. Bounded preparation yields100/73/73 on the frozen180 and27/22/20 on fresh90, with censoring separately recorded. The first full-match pairs retain policy harms and unrealistic mismatch scores. Ordinary clear keeper contacts survive the small clean-shot set, but difficult conditions lose saves.

No component receives a success bonus for making more highlights, increasing completion or creating a larger score. The next dependency is sensory acquisition/cadence versus motor effort/preparation, with real early/later contacts and physical continuation tested independently. Full-world policy/tactical information remains separately registered work.

Future changes must preserve the executable profile, explicit dependencies and negative controls; rerun relevant local and policy-specific full matches; record the per-mechanism and whole-profile decisions; and regenerate progress without weakening original I/F/E/T/M/C or opening independent confirmation prematurely.
