## Confirmed mechanical causes

The actual call chain integrates controlled-ball touches before ranking the next
action. Routine dribble contact repeatedly consumes the foot window and restarts
motor recovery. Pass candidates exist but are excluded by `canReleaseControlledAction`.
In the native 39m retreat, only 2/79 evaluations originally have release access.
R3's build-up/outlet value then keeps a short retreat competitive among the remaining
carry/hold alternatives. Phase/role value reinforces the escape; it is not evidence
that every backward step deserves a penalty. The correction gives an already
executable decision one physical tick before another automatic dribble touch.
The ball still rolls; actual reach, preparation, opposition and normal action ranking
remain authoritative. There is no forced pass, time-in-possession cap or territory ban.

Native R3 retreat distances change approximately **17/14/29/39m → 4/9/7/4m**.
All four corrected continuations regain feasible forward passing alternatives.
Partners in these native episodes move at mean speeds 1.4–2.1m/s before the repair,
with many target changes and substantial displacement. These traces do not support
literal whole-team immobility. The original sampled export cannot prove individual
teammate velocities; conclusions about it remain bounded by that limitation.

The substitution model counted temporary burst depletion both in role suitability
and as a freshness benefit, then accepted small positive gains. A reproduced
147.75s replacement has benefit only +0.81: role +6.60, fatigue +6.90, ability −3.89,
opportunity cost −8.80. Sustainable role fit now separates the recoverable burst
reserve, observed role evidence affects confidence, and unused changes/windows carry
remaining-duration and marginal scarcity value. Emergency injury and genuine large
early tactical improvements remain possible through the legal Law 3 lifecycle.

Carry pursuit previously required `humanSelected` and the controlled ID. NPCs now
retain the same finite reach/arrival chase after their own deliberate touch.
Movement arbitration respects that chase without granting possession or excluding
an opponent. A different touch, release, unreachable ball, explicit new movement or
expired intention still invalidates it.

Tackling reach could be treated as established control even for a stretched,
fast-moving poke. Such a contact now remains a deflection/loose ball when it cannot
be trapped within the normal feet/speed envelope. Its last-touch identity belongs
to the defender. Acquisition also checks a rival arriving during preparation,
rather than relying on the rival nomination from its start.

Settled goalkeeper depth was the only ground-carrier positioning authority.
The keeper now proposes angle reduction for an uncovered approaching attacker,
using visible geometry, available cover, attributes and arrival estimates. Existing
loose-ball sweeping, aerial claims and physical shot responses remain separate.
No reach or save probability is inflated. Boundary checks keep this advisory target
within the pitch and leave actual out-of-play adjudication to the canonical resolver.

The existing control-completion acknowledgement remains stable when a keeper advances.
A natural restart also closes the previous visible consequence after its readable tail,
instead of merging the next dangerous restart into the old pass until a safety bound.
These are presentation/agency consequences; they do not alter match-clock time.
