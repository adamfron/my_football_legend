# PR162 — Integrated Match Realism, Tactical Flow and Formation Geometry Audit

This change makes attacking failures measurable and corrects confirmed decision/geometry defects. It does not establish that MFL's football balance is solved. Offensive creation remains sparse in the equal-attribute experiment; the PR should be reviewed as an audit and causal correction, with the remaining funnel bottleneck visible.

2. Build independent legal formation fixtures and an observational attacking funnel; establish controlled and matched-seed baselines.
3. Change only mechanisms supported by geometry, ranking or physical traces; test readiness, agency, contact, restarts and deterministic identity.

## Confirmed causes and implemented corrections

**Decision cadence:** `npcPossessionDecisionDelay` compared a tactical `fieldValue` score against metre-like danger thresholds. At x=84, y=34 a forward only 21 m from goal still received approximately five seconds of mandatory waiting. Perception now uses actual goal distance, overlaps the incoming pass's usable awareness time, and is bounded by the existing physical first-touch readiness. Pressure and transition urgency shorten perception, never physical control. Ranking still expresses tactical patience. First-time contacts keep their own existing physical resolver.

**Depth responsibility:** the old temporary-run threshold rejected an average support-duty forward solely because he stood more than about 15 m from the carrier. Build-up support could then consume the forward's job. A possession-dependent relational depth assignment reserves one forward to stretch the line; an already advanced winger/midfielder can compensate when a lone forward drops. Targets respect current onside depth, bounded distance ahead of the carrier, reading ability and ordinary locomotion. Another forward/support player retains the connection job. No pass success, teleport or tactical team bonus is added.

**Duplicated pass penalties and incompatible race clocks:** pass ranking counted every defender within a static 3.5 m segment corridor after the physical forecast had already accounted for attainable interception and endpoint pressure. It also judged through-ball context using instant-top-speed estimates. Ranking now uses the physical forecast's lane occupation and receiver/defender ETA. Reachable-run forecasts use existing reserve-dependent movement capacity and acceleration/turn arrival estimates. Actual pass error, reception, offside and interception resolvers are unchanged.

**Impossible restart geometry:** in a pristine `pr162-1` club match, a secondary teammate occupied the ball transport endpoint. The carrier remained about 0.11 m from its body target and kept moving, so placement could not legally complete. Clearing every nearby teammate's target released the preserved actual state in 2.425 seconds. A second full-match trace showed a 0.85 m wall fighting the canonical 1.15 m body separation, with non-wall markers projected onto the same legal-distance arc. Wall spacing and stand-off targets now leave physical room. Legal readiness, stopped-ball requirements, award identity, timekeeping and human restart control remain authoritative; there is no timeout success or forced whistle.

The preliminary V3 pilot exposed another instance at a goal kick on x=0: an outward staging target was clamped back into the placement body's corridor. The preserved `4-2-3-1:pr162-1:true` state, awarded at 5151.175 s and still blocked at the 7200 s horizon, reproduces the defect. Inward staging releases its same frozen state after 4.025 s. A regression test first failed with only 1.007 m of target separation, then passed with the ordinary placement/release path. Final experiments use this additional geometry correction; prior source trials are retained separately, never pooled into the final matrix.

**Physical contact versus painted-pitch coordinates:** long club tests exposed a chain of schema errors for a legal ball centre partly beyond the touchline. Ground-claim, receiver-readiness, reception outcome and actual-contact diagnostics now retain finite physical points. Intended tactical targets remain pitch-bounded. Boundary adjudication still checks the whole ball, and the contact is resolved with the existing real-body/foot model. Neither clamping nor magnetic recovery is introduced. A late replay also exposed the same invalid pitch bounds on threat-memory solution origins and control origins. These two descriptors now accept finite physical points; two real touchline-pass tests first failed and then passed. This validation-only patch follows the frozen matrix source, so its separate two-full-match complete-state parity receipt must accompany the main evidence.

## Controlled evidence and limitations

Nine scenarios × three fixed seeds cover the line gap, dropping striker, two-forward staggering, open lane, pressure escape, edge of the box, unforced rebound, fresh run and depleted reserves. Each starts from a recorded frozen geometry and then advances 800 canonical ticks. The edge-box fixture changed from one total shot to five across the three seeds, and its decision delay from 5.04 s to 0.40 s. The rebound-labelled fixture shares that initial geometry and is not independent evidence of a forced save, rebound or corner. All full rankings, physical executability, initial opportunity and bounded action traces are retained.

Earlier ten-minute paired 4-4-2 probes showed that cadence alone increased circulation without producing a shot. The combined corrections produced more progressive/through attempts and entries in those windows, but still zero shots. Therefore faster action alone is not a sufficient football-flow fix. We deliberately retain this failed hypothesis rather than interpreting higher passing volume as offensive success.

Full club and formation results determine the practical remaining scope. Native squads, coach styles, active XI/bench and effective strengths are preserved in the real-club files: Błękitni's selected XI has effective strength 79, 4-3-3 and balanced style; Górnik's has strength 72, 4-4-2 and pressing style. These are actual selected-XI strengths, not simply a headline OVR. Equal-attribute fixtures isolate geometry plus native duties, not geometry alone. A normalised-duty sensitivity run and controlled ability perturbation are reported separately; they cannot establish a stable global quality ordering from a small sample.

## Acceptance questions

1. **Where does the funnel fail?** The original observation loses progression between final-third circulation and box entry; the pilot table separates controlled advancement, sustained final presence, credible opportunity and actual release.
2. **Why hundreds of passes without shots?** Delayed decisions let defenders recover; depth jobs disappear, progressive candidates carry duplicated risk penalties, and scarce dangerous control still favours retention. The remaining ranking/geometry interaction is not fully solved.
3. **Why few striker passes?** Run eligibility and support-job consumption suppress depth availability, while marker/race penalties suppress selection. Tables distinguish candidates, attempts and actual receptions.
4. **Do forwards threaten the line?** Actual ten-second line-gap moments are reported. Assignment tests establish legal targets; they do not prove that every forward reaches them or receives a pass.
5. **Compensation when dropping?** A deterministic lone-striker test confirms an advanced winger receives the compensating job. Controlled traces show subsequent movement; full-match compensation frequency is not fully instrumented.
6. **Can line-breaking passes occur?** Canonical through completions and legal receptions beyond the release-time line are measured. Midfield-line breaking needs a separate clustering definition.
7. **Incorrect rejection?** The duplicated static corridor and incompatible ETA penalty were confirmed. The correction does not presume every rejected forward pass is good.
8. **Cadence too slow?** Yes in the 21 m fixture: 5.04 s became 0.40 s, while the physical readiness floor remains tested.
9. **Possession leading to a shot?** Yes, unforced edge-box play produced five shots across three seeds. The 120-match matrix measures how rarely teams reach that setup.
10. **Saves/rebounds/corners?** Canonical totals and existing goalkeeper/contact tests are retained. Twelve requested-shot geometries keep the potential rebound attacker onside and produce five initial saves/four initial parries and two endogenous same-attack follow-ups per source. These are conditional physical-mechanism probes, not initial AI selection evidence or corner-rate calibration.
11. **Defenders too effective?** No evidence supports globally weakening tackling, interception or recovery. Impossible preparation geometry was fixed; ordinary defensive execution remains.
12. **Contextual high press?** Dedicated baseline pressing fixtures and challenge zones provide limited evidence. A causal press-choice advantage over other styles is not established.
13. **Space after escaping pressure?** The controlled fixture records the geometry and outcomes. A complete escape-to-shot transition denominator remains open.
14. **Retention overvalued?** It remains a plausible ranking bottleneck after the confirmed duplicated penalty is removed. Increasing arbitrary shot preference is not evidence of a fix.
15. **Pirouettes?** Measured duration after >360 degrees with <3 m net displacement appears in the shape table. This is a bounded observational definition, not a claim that all slow carries are defective.
16. **Overprecise recovery?** Position standard deviations and target/anchor distances test real movement. No formation-snap mechanism was added; exact offensive recovery timing remains unmeasured.
17. **Foul zones?** Observed ground-challenge fouls are tabulated in the committing team direction; this is not a complete spatial census of every foul source. Canonical total fouls remain separate. Sparse samples cannot validate a league-wide distribution.
18. **Fatigue/substitutes?** Physical reserve sensitivity is tested and fresh/depleted runs are sampled; actual substitution identities/workload stay canonical. Tactical substitution effectiveness is not established.
19. **Does 4-4-2 dominate?** No general superiority claim follows from 12 seed blocks per matchup and only 36 total shots. Some paired shot intervals exclude zero, but five exploratory comparisons and a self-match identity asymmetry prevent treating them as a formation ranking.
20. **4-4-2 symmetry?** The equal 4-4-2 self-match is asymmetric: anchor W/D/L 1/17/6 and shots 0.08/0.46 per match; paired anchor-minus-opponent shots −0.38, exploratory 95% interval [−0.61, −0.14]. Reversal alone does not remove this logical-identity/initialisation signal. Its cause is unresolved; it must not be attributed to formation superiority.
21. **Most forward availability?** Formation-specific striker attempts/receptions and gaps are reported; no ranking beyond observed fixtures is asserted.
22. **Most progressive options?** Progressive releases/completions, through candidates and actual receptions are shown. Candidate samples are not every decision.
23. **Any formation unable to shoot?** Zero observed shots means no shot in these fixtures, not mathematical impossibility. The table explicitly retains zero-producing matchups.
24. **Geometry or duties?** Primary results combine geometry and native duties. The sensitivity configuration separates duties; a wider paired ablation is needed for attribution.
25. **Robust across seed blocks?** All block values and uncertainty are retained. Twelve blocks per matchup provide exploratory distributions, not robust global superiority; sparse scoring and self-match asymmetry remain material limits.
26. **Why Górnik can outperform Błękitni?** The stored club ratings (78/71), selected XI (79/72) and whole-squad primary OVR (75.79/68.50) are distinct projections. Native role attributes, XI/bench condition and coach formation/style defaults are recorded. Six matched orientations have only two total shots, so they cannot explain a stable quality ordering. No club-ID bonus was added.
27. **Consistency or seed variation?** The native-club mirrored experiment supplies the observed distribution; incomplete/censored baseline pairs cannot support a stable ordering.
28. **Controlled ability advantage?** 50/50, 60/50, 70/50, 80/60 and reversed 50/70 mirrored ten-minute windows measure passing/progression/workload. A separate full 70/50 pair gives W/D/L 1/1/0 and shots 2.00/0.00. One full seed pair and short windows do not establish monotonic win rates.
29. **Technical superiority to danger?** That is still limited by access to useful space and action selection. Raw pass completion alone is not dangerous possession.
30. **ID/orientation/init bias?** Stable logical IDs, fresh profiles, complete hashes and reversal audit the main paths. The measured equal-4-4-2 logical-identity asymmetry requires explicit ID/permutation and initialisation probes next. Home kickoff remains explicit; the cause has not been identified.
31. **Confirmed root causes?** Misused territory units, duplicate reception waiting, absent average-forward depth responsibility, duplicated lane/ETA penalties, impossible placement/wall targets and physical-coordinate schema rejection.
32. **Disproved hypotheses?** Cadence alone did not create shots in the matched ten-minute window. The original export disproves the idea that the teams simply cannot complete passes. No evidence justifies a universal defensive nerf.
33. **Implemented changes?** Contextual perception, relational depth/compensation, shared physical forecasts, achievable restart targets, physical contact schemas, legal fixtures, independent funnel, checkpointed CLI, aggregation and tests.
34. **Measured improvement?** Controlled edge-box shooting and physical restart recovery improved. The tables retain progression/entry changes and full-match limits; no global success rate is invented.
35. **Regressions?** Long runs exposed the boundary reception chain and restart packing defects; both are covered. Earlier source trials are not mixed into the final matrix. The later threat-memory descriptor repair has separate complete-state parity and real-pass regression coverage. All 120 main matches finish; low shot volume and self-match asymmetry remain.
36. **Deterministic?** All 80 exported telemetry fields are compared in full twice per seed/revision, with schema/invariant and identity-ledger checks. Public receipts contain inventories and repeat hashes; full payloads remain local. Observer and late-schema parity compare complete canonical state, not only scores.
37. **Agency/physics preserved?** Shot/restart control and human/NPC physics suites remain in the full gate. Physical effort parity now isolates the same committed action from the intentionally faster next NPC decision.
38. **Open issues?** Sparse dangerous progression, realistic shot volume, meaningful pressure-escape conversion, full defensive-line clustering, wider duty/ability ablations and formation bias uncertainty.
39. **Next experiments?** Inspect rejected progressive/terminal actions in sustained final-third possessions; then probe logical-ID/initialisation asymmetry and run wider matched duty/geometry and ability ablations. Avoid changing several ranking weights together.
40. **Broader plausible football?** The engine can now act sooner and finish previously impossible preparation, but 120 completed matches do not establish generally realistic offensive football. This is progress toward that goal, with its remaining failure measurable.

## Engineering and follow-ups

No second engine, statistical quotas, compensating team win probabilities or forced corner/rebound outcomes were introduced. The observer is not imported by canonical simulation. Normalised fixtures use real schemas and the native action/contact/physics paths. Public machine results contain numerical moments, role attribute means, orientation/seed-block distributions and source/validation receipts. Complete profiles, identities, bounded traces and the two frozen restart states are retained in the local evidence bundle; no per-tick trajectory archive is required. See [data retention](performance/PR162-DATA_README.md).

The stadium configuration is a deterministic function of home-club identity and kit, independent of match seed. Presentation remains outside football adjudication. More substantial player-model revision, populated substitute benches and simple 1990s billboard/sprite spectators remain separate presentation follow-ups.

The complete `npm run verify` passed on GitHub for canonical source `e652c31113fe3406d5198c6660811ebd820d8c99`: 1,521 general tests in 183 files, all 29 tests in two serial match/renderer integration suites, and five full-career tests; lint and production build also passed ([CI receipt](https://github.com/adamfron/my_football_legend/actions/runs/38103355504)). CI scheduling changes preserve each suite's individual timeouts and assertions. Shared-runner contention required one worker and separate integration scheduling; a subsequent ten-minute whole-job ceiling interrupted the career suite, so that job budget is now fifteen minutes. This changes scheduling/resource budget, not acceptance conditions.

Behavioural test expectations do change where the intended cadence changes: old mandatory five-second scanning constants are replaced by the real preparation floor and actual action availability. The delegated finishing test now expects the earlier mandatory human shot opportunity while still rejecting an autonomous shot. Physical effort parity holds the same committed action during its comparison window so a newly available next NPC decision cannot confound locomotion/fitness parity. Complete telemetry reference hashes are updated only after independently reproducing both pristine and corrected all-field outputs twice per seed. These updates document changed behaviour; CI resource fixes do not shorten test trajectories or relax physical/agency invariants.

Runtime/observer costs are recorded with the evidence. Observer overhead median +2.40% and step-cost change median +3.09% have broad mean uncertainty intervals; six short alternating pairs do not establish renderer FPS or a precise slowdown. A green gate validates implementation; football-balance conclusions remain limited by the measured coverage.
