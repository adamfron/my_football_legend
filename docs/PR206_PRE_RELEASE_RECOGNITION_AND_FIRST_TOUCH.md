## Physical mediation, subsequent touches and uncertainty

A predeclared subset (sample 0, longer window, fast ground / lateral / pressure) performs two more same-ball continuations. Both arms have reading 85, touch 20 and the same high-reading optical memory, destination and other world state. Only position, velocity, facing, plant balance and optional physical pose/jump fields come from the actual low- versus high-reading prelude. This tests the contribution of the already prepared body independently of post-release reading and knowledge.

These three pairs do **not** add a control or retained-possession success from the prepared body. The pressure pair controls/retains in both arms, whereas the original LL versus HL factorial differs. Thus later lawful cognition/movement can recover the low-reading body in this apparatus; the original difference does not establish that pre-release bodily preparation was necessary. The fast pair fails control in both; the lateral pair controls/retains in both with a one-tick timing shift. Changed physical difficulty or earlier touch alone is not credited as cognitive–technical compensation.

The receipts preserve real misses, finite contacts with heavy/failed control, later control after earlier opponent touch, next decisions/releases, and later poor touches. Direct touch plus control, team ownership one second after touch, uninterrupted retention and useful subsequent action remain different outcomes. A completed first-time action is not inferred from ownership: the factorial records no first-time redirection. A separate repeated PR204 authority control executes an actual finite first-time pass at 10.850 s (native incoming approximately (4.679, −0.614) m/s, outgoing (−4.623, −1.600) m/s), including human/defensive/restart negative counterparts. That is an execution/authority positive control, not a new anticipation success or proof of a useful completed next reception.

Full eight-second delivery outcomes are not censored in this corpus. The explicitly separate 0.25 s right-censoring sensitivity retains all 96 still-unobserved outcomes as censored rather than failures. It uses only canonical samples up to that horizon and is never pooled with the primary denominator. Misses ending in another player's control are genuine observed failures, not censors.

Only **two underlying seed clusters** underlie the repeated families/windows. Frames, factorial arms and technical checkpoint interventions are dependent. Paired wins/losses and per-seed mean differences are descriptive sensitivity measures; no narrow population confidence interval, calibrated skill curve or universal ordering is justified. Cases where higher technique loses retention or higher reading fails to help remain visible. In particular, this study does not establish that high reading/low touch generally outperforms low reading/high touch.

The `long_ground` label covers the measured release distances in the numerical appendix. The prospective hypothesis requires intermediary distance ≤24 m and detailed observation ≤36 m, so this apparatus does not cover genuinely distant blind long-pass recognition. The descending condition is exploratory and excluded from the primary counts. High/aerial equivalence must be validated independently before extending this conclusion.
